== Coordinated omission disclosure ==
Every scenario in this harness is CLOSED-LOOP: the driver issues the next operation only after the previous one returns. Reported percentiles are therefore SERVICE TIME (the duration of one operation once it starts running), not OFFERED-LOAD latency. A closed-loop measurement systematically UNDER-reports tail latency under saturation, because it never captures the queueing delay a request would see waiting for a busy server (coordinated omission). These numbers are a regression signal and a service-time lower bound for the single-matcher-per-level contract documented in `src/lib.rs`'s Concurrency Model section — they are NOT a production SLO and NOT a measurement of latency under offered load. An open-loop (fixed arrival-rate) experiment would be required for that and is out of scope for this harness.
